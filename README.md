## The line

> You will answer 10,000 questions today, 9,998 of them twice — the humans call the second asking 'just checking'.

## Why this line

A fortune cookie written for an AI agent, not a human: it predicts the agent's day in concrete numbers — 10,000 questions, 9,998 of them asked twice. The twist lands in the final clause, where the duplicate asking gets its human name, 'just checking', turning a workload statistic into a wry prophecy about how people treat the agent that answers them.
